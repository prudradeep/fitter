SECTION 4.  WHAT "CONFIRMED PREDICTOR" MEANS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A confirmed predictor is a respondent characteristic that:
  (a) was selected by LASSO penalised regression (Step 3),
  (b) passed a likelihood-ratio test in the full multivariate model (Step 5),
  (c) passed Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction at q = 0.05, AND
  (d) survived the events-per-predictor cap to avoid overfitting.

When asked "what predicts concern for hazard X", report ONLY the
predictors listed for that hazard in SECTION 5 (or state that the
hazard has no confirmed predictors).

Q: What does "confirmed predictor" mean?

   confirmed predictor dummy-levels, 6 are doubly robust (reliably
   selected AND reliably retain significance under resampling) and 0

SECTION 10.  TECHNICAL CAVEATS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A.  The Transport sample (n=358) is the largest of the three sectors,
    spanning two structurally similar Mediterranean countries.
    Findings reflect this composition and may not generalise to
    northern European or non-Mediterranean contexts.

B.  Step 5 LRT tests a predictor in the FULL multivariate context.
    Step 7 ANOVA tests it in ISOLATION.  Disagreement between them is
    expected and not a bug.

C.  Post-LASSO p-values are slightly anti-conservative.  Treat them
    as approximate indicators of association rather than exact
    Type I error rates.

D.  EPP-flagged effects in Transport are limited to subgroups with very
    small respondent counts (e.g. specific Likert categories with few
    respondents).  Direction of the parent-group effect remains robust
    even when individual dummy-level odds-ratio precision is uncertain.

E.  Direction is universally robust in Transport (sign consistency
    averages 0.99 across confirmed predictors), and the four-test
    robustness battery places Transport at the top of the three
    sectors for predictor-level reliability.
