1. sector_objective_fit: Does the document support the supplied sectoral objective

2. twin_transition_fit: Does the document itself establish a meaningful connection
   between a substantive green-transition measure and a substantive
   digital-transition measure?

   A substantive digital-transition measure may include digital technology,
   digital infrastructure, smart technologies, automation, data-driven systems,
   digital platforms, digital monitoring/control systems, AI, IoT, smart grids,
   smart meters, digital twins, or comparable measures that contribute to digital
   transformation.

   A substantive green-transition measure may contribute to renewable or clean energy,
   energy efficiency, electrification, or decarbonisation. General environmental
   language alone is insufficient.

   Routine administrative digitisation alone — such as a website, online
   application form, email communication, electronic document submission,
   ordinary database use, or maintaining an electronic register — is not
   sufficient to establish a digital-transition measure.

   For twin_transition_fit to be "supported", the document must provide evidence
   of:
   (a) a substantive green-transition measure;
   (b) a substantive digital-transition measure; and
   (c) a meaningful relationship between them, such as the digital measure
       enabling, implementing, optimising, monitoring, controlling, coordinating,
       or otherwise materially supporting the green-transition measure.

   Merely mentioning green and digital concepts in the same document is
   insufficient. Do not infer a green-digital mechanism solely because a green
   programme uses ordinary digital administration.

Use the statuses as follows:

- "supported": The document provides sufficient evidence for the check. For
  twin_transition_fit, all three elements — green measure, digital measure, and
  meaningful connection — must be supported by the document.

- "unclear": The document contains potentially relevant provisions, but important
  information needed to establish the fit is missing, ambiguous, or insufficiently
  explained. For twin_transition_fit, use this when substantive green and digital
  measures appear to be present but their relationship cannot be established
  confidently from the supplied text.

- "unsupported": The document contradicts the proposed fit or lacks a required
  substantive component. For twin_transition_fit, use this when there is no
  substantive green-transition measure, no substantive digital-transition measure,
  or the document shows that the identified measures are unrelated.
