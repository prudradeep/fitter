Financial strain comes first

In every sector, people already under financial pressure see transition hazards as more serious.

Shared worries need broad answers

The most widely felt hazards, such as higher bills, are hardest to link to one group. They call for measures that reach everyone.

Circumstances matter more than country

Country explains only 1–4% of differences in concern. Measures can be designed around people's situations and applied across countries.

These results describe how surveyed people perceive hazards, not harm that has been measured. Odds compare a group with otherwise similar people outside it.
