What is aHazard?

In a twin transition policy context, a hazard is a potential negative consequence that can arise when a policy for the green and/or digital transition is implemented.

What does a hazard mean?

A hazard is a potential harm, risk, or negative impact that may affect people, communities, the economy or the environment as a result of a twin transition policy.
