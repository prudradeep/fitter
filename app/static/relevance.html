Hazard Relevance: What It Is and How It’s Calculated

Relevance

A composite score that tells how important a hazard is for a country–region, combining public concern, association strength, and regional exposure.

Relevance (h, c, r)= wS ·Salience (h, c)+ wE ·EffectSize (h)+ wR ·Reach (h, c, r)
👥

Salience (h, c)

How strongly people in a country are concerned about the hazard.

Salience (h, c) = mean concern score × (high-concern % / 100)
