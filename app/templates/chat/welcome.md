👋 Welcome to **Dr Transition**, the FITTER-EU conversational digital coach for reasoning about the social and distributional effects of green-and-digital transition policies in Europe.

Dr transition helps you work through a local policy context step by step. You will choose a country, region, sector and set a policy context; identify relevant hazards and affected population groups; co-develop mitigation measures; use evidence to support inputs; and reflect on the feasibility and transformative impact of the mitigation.

Dr Transition is an **assistant**, not an automatic decision-maker. It does not simply give a score or prescribe a policy. Instead, it aims to help sharpen and document your own reasoning by asking which socio-demographic groups may be disadvantaged, what evidence supports a claim, and how a mitigation measure could be made fairer and more defensible.`
